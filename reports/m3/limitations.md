# M3 limitations

Generated by `openinspect dedup analyze` from `artifacts/m3/audit.json` (re-render with `openinspect dedup report`). Rules: [docs/M3_PROTOCOL.md](../../docs/M3_PROTOCOL.md) (frozen before any leakage number was computed) and [docs/M3_PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT.md](../../docs/M3_PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT.md). Code: `a030e7b111f1`; seed 0.

A *visual similarity component* (a potential leakage group) is a connected component of image pairs whose embedding cosine reaches a threshold: evidence of potential leakage, not proof that two images show the same physical object. No image was deleted, excluded or re-labelled.

What the M3 numbers cannot say, with the measurement behind each point.

1. **Proxy labels, not ground truth.** The thresholds come from the sources' own keys (production batch, design family: proxy metadata) and from synthetic copies. The pool results are group-label agreement, not duplicate accuracy; precision 0.90 was never reached in `pcb-defect` family A, `pcb-defect` (A, B), `pcb-ind` (batch, side), so the rule fell back to the F1-optimal cosine. The human review queue (`artifacts/m3/review-candidates.csv`) has no decision yet.
2. **Chaining.** Components are transitive: A ~ B and B ~ C put A and C in one component however unlike they are. Flagged at the family level: `pcb-defect`. At the near level the largest component still holds `pcb-defect` 48.3% of its source, so that source's groups are chains rather than groups of duplicates.
3. **Synthetic recall.** The synthetic copies are mild transforms of a seeded sample; they measure sensitivity to re-encoding, resampling and small crops, not to re-photographing. At the final near threshold every source keeps at least the target share of its copies (`dspcbsd-plus` 95.7%, `pcb-defect` 99.5%, `pcb-ind` 95.0%).
4. **Uncertainty.** The protocol's intervals resample the positive groups and keep the negative pairs fixed. The both-sides check gives a materially different width for `pcb-defect` family A precision at family (2.17); `pcb-defect` (A, B) precision at family (2.41), so those intervals understate the uncertainty.
5. **Representation.** Every image is squeezed whole to 224x224. Large scans lose most of their pixels: `pcb-defect` (median longer side 2,942 px, up to 5,971). The second representation (`facebook/dinov2-base@f9e44c814b77`) gives the same random-split reading for 4 of 4 source and level pairs, so the direction of the findings holds; the size does not (at the family level the second representation finds 83 crossing components in `dspcbsd-plus` against 216, and 4.7% of its evaluation images with a training neighbour against 23.5%; at the family level the second representation finds 7 crossing components in `pcb-ind` against 57, and 0.8% of its evaluation images with a training neighbour against 17.2%), because each representation reaches its own thresholds by the rule and the second's near threshold keeps less than the target share of the synthetic copies of `dspcbsd-plus`, `pcb-ind` ([representation-robustness.md](representation-robustness.md)).
6. **Leakage is not a score.** M3 measures the structure of the data; no model was trained. Later, A0 - A1 (random against group-aware split of the same sources) approximates the effect of split leakage, and A1 - B (group-aware against source-held-out) a residual source or domain shift. A0 - B is not leakage: acquisition hardware, factory, lighting, resolution, annotation style, taxonomy and label distribution all differ between sources.
7. **Independence of the sources.** 115 pairs between `dspcbsd-plus` and `pcb-ind` reach the family threshold, so a source-held-out split does not hold out every look-alike.
8. **Sampling of the review queue.** The queue is stratified, not random: its counts do not estimate how common each category is in the data.
9. **Recorded performance.** The two runs that did the embedding work predate the per-run record; their times are transcribed from the console logs (amendment N5).
