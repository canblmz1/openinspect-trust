# M4 class overlap

Generated by `openinspect taxonomy audit` from `artifacts/m4/audit.json` and `configs/taxonomy.yaml` (re-render with `openinspect taxonomy report`). Code: `6ba0c9e075f1`; taxonomy SHA-256 `b8a8357314d4`.

Original labels are never changed: every box keeps the label its source gave it, and the normalized label is added next to it (`artifacts/m4/taxonomy-map.parquet`). A mapping rests on the source's paper and documentation and on sampled boxes, not on the label's name; the definitions and the reasoning are in [docs/TAXONOMY.md](../../docs/TAXONOMY.md).

**Answer.** 4 classes can be compared across all 3 independent sources without silently changing their meaning: `short`, `open`, `mouse_bite`, `spurious_copper` (an EXACT or COMPATIBLE mapping in every source). Of the 12 mappings behind them, 2 are COMPATIBLE rather than EXACT (`pcb-defect` `mouse_bite` to `mouse_bite`; `dspcbsd-plus` `SC` to `spurious_copper`); their differences in scope are part of the evidence in [taxonomy-mapping.md](taxonomy-mapping.md).

Shared by some sources but not all, so outside the cross-source benchmark: `spur` (`dspcbsd-plus`, `pcb-defect`); `scratch` (`dspcbsd-plus`, `pcb-ind`).

Every other class is source-specific or ambiguous and keeps its own name; no label was merged to enlarge the overlap.
