# M4 label quality

Generated by `openinspect taxonomy audit` from `artifacts/m4/audit.json` and `configs/taxonomy.yaml` (re-render with `openinspect taxonomy report`). Code: `6ba0c9e075f1`; taxonomy SHA-256 `b8a8357314d4`.

Original labels are never changed: every box keeps the label its source gave it, and the normalized label is added next to it (`artifacts/m4/taxonomy-map.parquet`). A mapping rests on the source's paper and documentation and on sampled boxes, not on the label's name; the definitions and the reasoning are in [docs/TAXONOMY.md](../../docs/TAXONOMY.md).

**Human validation: NOT PERFORMED.** Reviewed pairs: 0 / 300 (`artifacts/m3/review-candidates.csv`). The similarity findings have not yet been independently human-validated. Thresholds are based on proxy metadata, synthetic transforms and representation-based similarity. The near-duplicate check below uses the M3 pairs.

No label, box or image was changed or dropped. Each finding is one row of `artifacts/m4/review-required.csv` with status `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and empty `human_decision` and `human_notes` columns: 437 rows. A finding asks a person to look; it is not an error count.

## Rules

| signal | check | rule |
|---|---|---|
| `malformed_box` | geometry | a coordinate is not finite, or the box is inverted |
| `zero_area_box` | geometry | the box has zero width or zero height |
| `box_out_of_bounds` | geometry | the box leaves the image (recorded at ingest) |
| `tiny_box` | geometry | the shorter side is below `min_box_side_px` |
| `extreme_aspect_ratio` | geometry | longer side over shorter side is above `max_aspect_ratio` |
| `duplicate_box` | class_consistency | two boxes with the same label overlap with IoU at or above `same_box_iou` |
| `conflicting_box` | class_consistency | two boxes with different labels overlap with IoU at or above `same_box_iou` |
| `class_size_outlier` | class_consistency | the modified z-score of the log relative box area within (source, label) is beyond `size_outlier_z` |
| `source_format_disagreement` | source_format | an ingest flag says the source's annotation formats disagree or one cannot be read |
| `near_duplicate_label_conflict` | neighbour_label | a machine-detected near-duplicate pair (M3 near level and pHash at or below the M3 candidate distance, or identical bytes) whose normalized label sets differ |
| `mapping_ambiguity` | mapping | the label's mapping is AMBIGUOUS, so its boxes are not merged |

Values: `min_box_side_px` 2, `max_aspect_ratio` 20, `same_box_iou` 0.9, `size_outlier_z` 3.5; M3 near level cosine 0.933905 and pHash distance at most 4; ingest flags taken as informational: `no_annotations`.
