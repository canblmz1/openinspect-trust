Explicit limitations:

1. Human validation of the M3 similarity findings: NOT PERFORMED. Every visual similarity component that A1, B and the C designs rely on is machine-detected potential leakage; no person has checked whether its images are duplicates.
2. The group-aware split is representation-sensitive: an A1 built from DINOv2-base components differs materially (27.4% of the items change split (threshold 10%); `pcb-defect` is in train/val under A1 and in train under A1-base). Results from A1, B and C hold for the DINOv2-small grouping; the base-built A1 would leave 266 small-level primary pairs across its splits.
3. Under DINOv2-base, C1 does not separate train and test completely: `C1-d0` keeps 35 base similar pairs between them (`dspcbsd-plus` 1, `pcb-defect` 33, `pcb-ind` 1); `C1-d1` keeps 22 base similar pairs between them (`pcb-defect` 22); `C1-d2` keeps 37 base similar pairs between them (`dspcbsd-plus` 1, `pcb-defect` 35, `pcb-ind` 1). The C contrast is defined by the DINOv2-small grouping, so C0 - C1 is reported per source and the part of a source with such pairs holds only under that grouping.
4. `pcb-defect`: one constraint group holds 859 of its 939 crops (verdict E). It has no A1 test item and no validation item in the C designs; the C designs test only crops outside that group (59, 60, 59 in designs 0, 1, 2), and the whole source is tested only when it is held out (B).
5. The C designs miss their targets for `pcb-defect` (exposed 27, control 8, val 188): too few groups small enough to be split or held out.
6. C0 and C1 training boxes per class differ slightly (design 0, C0 minus C1: open +5, short +6, spurious_copper +4) where no replacement with the same boxes per class was left.
7. Released items with an unreviewed M4 finding: 1 with a training-relevant finding and 42 with limitation-only findings (49 findings); no label was corrected.
8. Images without a defect are excluded from every regime (127 available, all PCB-IND hard negatives): false positives on clean patches are not measured.
9. 65 pHash-only candidate pairs lie across A1 train and test; they are not a similarity level of the M3 protocol and stay unconstrained (P2).
10. Source identity is trivially predictable (balanced accuracy 100.0% from image size and file format; 89.9% from box and colour statistics alone): B measures a large source and domain shift, not only generalization.
11. B-strict removes training items that share a constraint *directly* with a held-out item; 535 training items of the `dspcbsd-plus` fold remain linked transitively.

## The twelve questions

**1. Is M6 PASS?**

PASS. The 20-item YOLO Detection package imported with matching counts, classes and the supplied 10/5/5 split, and a frozen version kept it (reports/m6/evren-smoke-test.md).

**2. Is the full export structurally valid?**

Packages checked: `A0` valid, `A1` valid, `B-dspcbsd-plus` valid, `B-pcb-defect` valid, `B-pcb-ind` valid, `C0-d0` valid, `C1-d0` valid, `C0-d1` valid, `C1-d1` valid, `C0-d2` valid, `C1-d2` valid, `B-natural-dspcbsd-plus` valid, `B-natural-pcb-defect` valid, `B-natural-pcb-ind` valid (boxes that leave their image by less than half a pixel pass, at most 0.0113 px; see export-validation.md). Each was read by an in-repository parser that shares no code with the exporter and by the Ultralytics dataset checks.

**3. How many release items are affected by M4 review findings?**

437 findings: 0 FATAL, 1 TRAINING_RELEVANT, 48 LIMITATION_ONLY, 388 NOT_IN_RELEASE; released items affected: 1 training-relevant and 42 limitation-only.

**4. Can A0/A1 score differences be interpreted?**

Not as a leakage effect. A0 tests 438 items, A1 369; they share 38 test items, A0 trains on 254 of A1's test items, and A1 has no test item from pcb-defect. A0 - A1 stays descriptive.

**5. Is a paired/common evaluation design available?**

Yes: C0/C1 with one common test set of 407 items (194 probes exposed in C0, 213 controls) and one validation set; C0 exposes 194 test items through 103 crossing constraint groups, C1 exposes 0 (0 crossing). Three seeded designs exist.

**6. How representation-sensitive is A1?**

Materially: an A1 built from DINOv2-base components moves 1,212 items (27.4%) to another split, and would leave 266 small-level primary pairs across its splits; the committed A1 cuts 3 base constraint groups. In design 0, 35 DINOv2-base similar pairs lie between C1's train and test sets (`dspcbsd-plus` 1, `pcb-defect` 33, `pcb-ind` 1; 46 in C0).

**7. What is happening with the PCB-Defect giant component?**

Verdict E: B: chaining gap 0.222, diameter 9 edges; C: crop-level components give a largest group of 71 instead of the inherited 859.

**8. How predictable is source identity?**

Very predictable: 100.0% balanced accuracy from size and format, 89.9% from boxes and colour statistics (majority baseline 40.0%).

**9. What is B-natural?**

The held-out source is the whole test set; every other item is available for training and validation (group-aware), and naturally similar cross-source items are kept: `dspcbsd-plus` keeps 620 linked training items, `pcb-defect` keeps 0 linked training items, `pcb-ind` keeps 181 linked training items.

**10. What is B-strict?**

The M5 split B: the held-out source is the test set and training items that share a constraint directly with a held-out item are excluded: `dspcbsd-plus` excludes 85, `pcb-defect` excludes 0, `pcb-ind` excludes 181.

**11. What claims are allowed without human review?**

Human validation is NOT PERFORMED. Allowed: machine-detected potential leakage, visual similarity group, embedding-defined component, and measured differences between regimes defined by them. Not allowed: confirmed duplicate, confirmed leakage, same physical board, human-validated leakage.

**12. Can M7 EVREN training begin?**

TRAINING READY WITH EXPLICIT LIMITATIONS. Yes, with the explicit limitations above, once the maintainer approves the EVREN compute; the M7 plan (m7-plan.md) is not executed here.
