The two test sets share 38 items; A0 trains on 254 of A1's test items.

- A0 and A1 do not share their test sets: they are different evaluation sets of different size and composition.
- A1 has no PCB-Defect test item, A0 has; a pooled A0 - A1 difference therefore mixes leakage with a change of what is evaluated.
- A0 - A1 stays a descriptive comparison of two historical split regimes. The controlled estimand is C0 - C1 on one common test set.

## The paired common-evaluation design (C0 / C1)

**What changes between conditions?**

- C0 trains on the group-mates of the probe test items (the other members of their A1 constraint groups); C1 trains on replacements instead: the same number of items, of the same source and, where possible, the same boxes per class, from groups that touch no test or validation item.

**What remains constant?**

- the test set (probes and controls) and the validation set, item for item;
- the training-set size and the number of training items per source;
- the class mix of the training set (equal boxes per class wherever a mate has a replacement with the same boxes; the residual difference is reported);
- every other training item (the core);
- the release, the taxonomy, the image files, the training configuration and the seeds.

The test set has two parts. *Probes* are test items whose group-mates C0 trains on; *controls* are whole groups that neither condition trains on. C0 - C1 on the probes estimates the effect of training on group-mates of a test item; on the controls it should be close to zero, which makes them a negative control for the swap. Probes and controls are different kinds of items (probes sit in groups, controls are mostly singletons): each is compared between C0 and C1, never with the other.

Parameters (every design): test_share 0.1, val_share 0.2, exposed_fraction 0.5, probe_fraction 0.5, max_group 50. Groups larger than `max_group` stay whole in training.

In the condition tables, *M3 pairs train-test* counts M3 candidate pairs, by machine category, with one image in train and one in test. REVIEW_REQUIRED pairs are pHash-only (the M3 review and family levels coincide), and a SAME_FAMILY_OR_SCENE pair of a source whose primary level is near lies below that level; neither is a constraint, so C1 may hold them. The primary-level pairs are counted in the column before.

## Two source-held-out regimes

- **B-strict** (the M5 split `B-<source>`): the held-out source is the test set, and training items that share a constraint *directly* with a held-out item are excluded. It asks how a model generalizes when both source identity and the known machine-similarity overlap are removed.
- **B-natural** (new): the held-out source is the test set and every other item is available for training and validation (group-aware), including items a machine similarity component links to the held-out source. It asks how a model generalizes to an unseen acquisition source under naturally occurring cross-source similarity.

The historical name `B` stays the file name of B-strict.

*Training items linked to test*: training or validation items whose A1 constraint group (the transitive union of all constraints) also holds a test item. B-strict excludes only the items that share a single constraint with a held-out item, so transitive links can remain; B-natural keeps every link.
