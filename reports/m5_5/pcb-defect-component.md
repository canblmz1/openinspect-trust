## Rules of the verdict (fixed before the diagnostics ran)

- A (visually cohesive): every pair of scans in the largest primary-level component is itself at or above the threshold (all-pair minimum >= threshold).
- B (transitive chaining): the chaining gap (edge minimum minus all-pair minimum) is at least 0.05 and the diameter is at least 3 edges.
- C (crop-generation style): with components computed on the crops instead of inherited from the scans, the largest group (with crop parents, design families and identical files) is below 50% of the inherited group.
- D (representation artifact): the other model's largest scan component at its own primary threshold holds less than 25% of the scans, while the primary's holds more.
- E (mixture): two or more of B, C and D hold.

## Consequence

The group is not broken by hand to obtain nicer split ratios. Under the A1 grouping rule PCB-Defect cannot take part in every split: A1 holds it in train and validation only, the C designs hold it in train and test (one exposure group, one control group) and in no validation set, and it is tested as a whole only when it is held out (B).
