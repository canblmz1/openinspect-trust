## Reading

A1 and B are defined by the DINOv2-small components and are not replaced: the M3 protocol made small the primary representation before any result, and base misses more of the synthetic near-duplicates (M3). But the populations those splits produce depend on that choice, so every result of A1, B and the C designs is a result *under the DINOv2-small grouping*, and the M7 report has to say so. The C designs use the same grouping: their contrast is the effect of training on group-mates as the DINOv2-small components, the metadata keys and the crop parents define them, and the probes and controls are recorded item by item. The DINOv2-base pairs between their train and test sets (above) show how much of C1's separation also holds under the other representation.
