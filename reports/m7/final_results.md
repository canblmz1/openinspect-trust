## Scientific verdict

Rule note: the support and strength rules are code in `scripts/m7_evaluate.py`, written before the design-0 numbers were computed. The first rendering of this report checked neither the controls of the replication designs nor the probe - control contrast; both checks were added afterwards. They can only lower the rating, and they did (from strong to moderate).

**1. Did C0 outperform C1 on the common D0 test population?**

Yes: C0 scored higher on 3 of 3 seeds; the three-seed mean difference is +1.76 pp (95% CI +0.24 to +3.82; the interval excludes zero). Each single seed's interval includes zero.

**2. By how many mAP50-95 points on each seed?**

seed 0: +1.75 pp (CI -0.20 to +4.28); seed 1: +2.36 pp (CI -0.07 to +5.07); seed 2: +1.16 pp (CI -0.60 to +3.58).

**3. What was the 3-seed mean delta?**

+1.76 pp; between-seed standard deviation 0.60 pp.

**4. What is the paired 95% confidence interval?**

+0.24 to +3.82 pp for the three-seed mean: paired bootstrap over A1 constraint groups (1000 resamples, RNG seed 0, the same resamples for the three seeds). It reflects the sampling of the test set, not the variation between training seeds.

**5. What happened on probe examples?**

The probes carry the difference: mean +3.91 pp (CI +1.38 to +6.76; the interval excludes zero); seed 0 +3.65, seed 1 +6.34, seed 2 +1.74; C0 higher on 3 of 3 seeds.

**6. What happened on controls?**

Controls: mean -0.17 pp (CI -1.66 to +1.86; the interval includes zero); seed 0 -0.56, seed 1 -1.40, seed 2 +1.46. Probe minus control: +4.08 pp (CI +1.06 to +7.33; the interval excludes zero).

**7. Did D1 reproduce the direction?**

Yes for the overall score: +3.70 pp (CI +1.61 to +6.14); probes +3.94 pp (CI +0.87 to +7.90); controls +3.09 pp (CI +0.37 to +6.67), which also moved: in this design the gain is not confined to the probes; probe minus control +0.84 pp (CI -4.31 to +5.25). One seed.

**8. Did D2 reproduce the direction?**

Yes for the overall score: +2.69 pp (CI +0.66 to +4.72); probes +4.60 pp (CI +1.67 to +7.29); controls +0.02 pp (CI -2.30 to +2.87), so the gain sits on the probes as in design 0; probe minus control +4.58 pp (CI +0.56 to +8.03). One seed.

**9. Is the observed effect larger than empirical training noise?**

Yes, modestly: the three-seed mean (+1.76 pp) is 1.6 times the empirical noise floor (1.13 pp: the largest test difference between identically configured replicates or the between-seed SD). Single-seed overall differences (+1.75, +2.36, +1.16) are of the same order as that floor. The probe difference (+3.91 pp) is 3.5 times the floor.

**10. Does the evidence support the claim that similarity/group leakage can make benchmark performance overly optimistic?**

Yes, for this benchmark and model, with a qualification: in design 0 (three seeds) and design 2 the gain is concentrated on the test images whose machine-defined group-mates were in training, while unexposed controls did not move measurably; in design 1 (one seed) the controls improved as well, so not all of the C0 - C1 gain there is explained by exposure.

**11. How strong is that evidence?**

Moderate. For: the probe effect is positive in all three seeds and both replication designs, its three-seed interval excludes zero, the probe - control contrast excludes zero, and the controls of design 0 stay near zero. Against: the overall effect is small (about two points) and of the order of training noise for a single seed, and the controls of design 1 moved too.

**12. What limitations prevent a stronger claim?**

The groups are machine-detected and not human-validated, so the exposure is to machine-defined group-mates, not to verified duplicates; one architecture (YOLO11n), one domain (PCB defects) and one release; three training seeds for design 0 and one each for designs 1 and 2; EVREN training is not bit-for-bit reproducible; probes and controls are small subsets of different composition; the bootstrap covers test sampling, not training variation; the runs used a cosine learning-rate schedule and label smoothing 0.1, which the plan did not specify (identical in every run); PCB-Defect contributes few probes and controls and no validation item.

**13. What did source-held-out B experiments show?**

A large source shift: dspcbsd-plus held out: 16.37 (against 43.74 on A1's in-distribution items of that source); natural - strict -1.15 pp (CI -2.07 to -0.38); pcb-defect held out: 0.33 (against 47.62 on A0's in-distribution items of that source); pcb-ind held out: 19.17 (against 56.82 on A1's in-distribution items of that source); natural - strict +0.55 pp (CI -0.74 to +2.35). Keeping the machine-similar cross-source items (B-natural) did not raise held-out scores. These measure source and domain shift, not leakage, and they dwarf the C0 - C1 effect.

**14. What should go into the paper/technical report headline?**

In a controlled YOLO11n experiment, training on machine-detected group-mates of test images raised mAP50-95 on those images by +3.91 pp (95% CI +1.38 to +6.76, three seeds) while unexposed controls did not change (-0.17 pp): group leakage makes this PCB benchmark measurably but modestly optimistic, far less than the loss when a source is held out.

**15. Is another training run scientifically necessary?**

Not for the claim as tested: the planned matrix is complete (three seeds on design 0, one each on designs 1 and 2). Optional: two more seeds of design 1 would show whether its control gain is training noise. A human review of the groups would strengthen the interpretation more than more training.
