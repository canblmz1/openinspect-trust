## What this means

- The *phenomenon* (similarity or group leakage inflates test scores) is well established, from CIFAR to LPR to OCT to automotive.
- One prior result goes the other way: large-scale dedup reports found little effect on COCO detection. That fits OpenInspect's small overall effect (+1.8 pp).
- The closest *design* is the automotive leakage-injection study: a fixed test set and controlled leakage levels with a YOLO detector. OpenInspect differs in using naturally occurring, machine-detected groups, a within-test probe/control contrast, and a source-held-out arm. I could not verify that paper's seeds or uncertainty treatment.
- I found no prior PCB-specific controlled leakage experiment. Given the search limitations, "not found" is not "does not exist".
